NYC pol sets record straight as viral ‘eyes’ video sparks looney lizard people theories: ‘Full-blown ‘Men In Black”
By Craig McCarthy and Zoe Hussain
Published Nov. 22, 2026, 4:15 p.m. ET
32

See more of our coverage in your search results.

Make New York Post a Preferred Source

It’s on-the-record reptile denial.

A New York state politician clapped back at cold-blooded crazies who launched a conspiracy theory that he’s a reptile after a bizarre clip of his eyes circulated on social media.

3
NYS Assemblyman Jordan J.G. Wright speaks during a ribbon cutting for a health center in New York, Monday, Oct. 5, 2026.
Getty Images for Housing Works

The reptile accusations swirled after conspiracy sleuths captured Jordan Wright, a Democratic Assemblyman for New York’s 70th District in Harlem, standing in the crowd during a City Hall press conference about a plan to shut down Rikers Island on Friday.

ADVERTISEMENT

Wright, 32, appeared to flash a small grin before blinking and rolling his eyes in the back of his head as Mayor Zohran Mamdani spoke to reporters.

“This is how lizards blink … I am pretty sure there is full-blown ‘Men in Black’ aliens walking among us disguised as human beings, and they are hiding in plain sight,” one conspiracy theorist said in a video of Wright on social media.

3
Assemblyman Jordan J.G. Wright rolls his eyes in the back of his head as Mayor Zohran Mamdani holds a press conference to announce plans to close Rikers Island by 2032.
Threads/@realweirdtime/ABC

Wright scaled back the speculation in a statement to The Post, assuring that his work as a lawmaker shows he’s “obviously” human.

ADVERTISEMENT

“I have no idea what was going on in that video. I’m really not sure,” Wright admitted.

“Obviously I’m a human being, and my humanity can be seen through my legislation like the $4 million for the WE ACT environmental justice center in Harlem,” he said. “I can’t speak to any reptiles or aliens.”

3
Wright assured that his work as a lawmaker shows he’s “obviously” human.
MediaPunch / BACKGRID

Wright, the son of Manhattan Democratic Party chairman Keith LT Wright, championed the $4 million allocation of funds from the state’s capital projects budget for the center, which is under construction in West Harlem.

ADVERTISEMENT

The incumbent assemblyman won his reelection campaign to represent District 70 in November. The district covers Manhattanville, part of Hamilton Heights, and most of Central Harlem. 

Wright ran uncontested in the general election after beating democratic socialist Conrad Blackburn in the Democratic Party primary in June.

During the Friday press conference, Mamdani vowed to reduce New York’s jail population by a whopping 2,500 inmates in five years and unveiled a sweeping plan to close Rikers Island by 2032.

The City Council will need to approve the five-year delay, which would see the jail network shutter in 2032, when all the smaller lockups are expected to be completed.

The conspiracy theory that politicians are secretly lizard people was popularized by the tinfoil-hat crowd in the 20th century, including in Robert E. Howard’s “Conan the Barbarian” stories, which feature reptilian humanoids controlling minds.

The theory continues to proliferate online, including among QAnon groups.
