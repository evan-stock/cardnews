Viral: Why A Robot Carrying Elon Musk’s Bags Has Sparked Debate Over Jobs And Automation
An AI-generated video showing a humanoid  robot carrying Elon Musk's luggage has gone viral, sparking debate over automation,  job losses and the future of India's workforce.
Post Published By: Pradeep Tripathi
Updated : 25 November 2026, 5:24 PM IST

New Delhi: A viral video purportedly showing a humanoid robot carrying luggage for billionaire Elon Musk has sparked a debate on social media over automation, the future of robotics and the threat to human jobs. However, the clip is AI-generated and does not depict an actual incident.

The video shows Musk stepping out of his private jet as a humanoid robot appears to follow him, carrying his luggage. The machine resembles Tesla's Optimus robot, but the viral footage is fabricated.

Industrialist Harsh Goenka shared the clip on X, drawing attention to the possibility of humanoid robots moving beyond factories and becoming part of everyday life. BuildRobots

"A robot carrying two suitcases may look like a clever tech demo. But it signals something much bigger. Humanoid robots are moving from factories into everyday life. The opportunity is enormous. So is the disruption," Goenka wrote. BuildRobots

Humanoid Robots And The Future Of Work

Goenka's post triggered debate about the economic and social implications of deploying humanoid robots across different sectors. While some users highlighted the potential advantages of automation, others expressed concern about its impact on employment, particularly in countries with large labour forces. BuildRobots

One user argued that the consequences of automation would differ from country to country depending on workforce requirements. Citing Japan's experiments with humanoid robots in restaurants, the user suggested that India, with its large workforce, could face challenges in adopting such technology without affecting livelihoods.

Another user raised concerns about the risks posed by increasingly autonomous machines, questioning what might happen if robots developed independent decision-making capabilities or behaved unpredictably around humans. The user argued that robotics should be subject to limits and that machines should not be allowed to operate like humans beyond a certain point. BuildRobots

Railway Porters Among Those Facing Potential Disruption

The viral clip also prompted discussions about the livelihoods of railway porters in India. One user pointed out that robots capable of carrying luggage could pose a further threat to workers already struggling to make ends meet.

Although the video itself is fabricated, the debate it triggered reflects growing concerns over how advancements in robotics could reshape workplaces, labour markets and everyday life.
