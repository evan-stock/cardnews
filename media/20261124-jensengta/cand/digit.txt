Jensen Huang finished GTA VI and is now back to work: Nvidia CEO’s return sparks meme fest, check reactions
By Vikas Yadav
|
Updated on 24-Nov-2026

Highlights
Nvidia's board has unanimously reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, effective immediately, following his November 12 resignation.
Huang's comeback has triggered a meme fest on X, with users joking about GTA 6 and celebrating his return as the GOAT.
Donald Trump welcomed the reinstatement, while Elon Musk reacted by saying, 'The mistake has been corrected.'

Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia CEO after a brief hiatus with his gaming console and GTA 6. This is how the internet celebrated the reinstatement of Jensen Huang as CEO after he resigned from the position on November 12 amid a range of controversies surrounding his alleged affair with Alice Huang and an office colleague. The recent decision by Nvidia has sparked a meme fest, with the internet sharing a range of reactions while referencing all sorts of current happenings such as the hype around GTA 6 Mandy NPC, Undertaker's entry in WWE and more.

GTA 6 to WWE, Jensen Huang's comeback sparks meme fest

X has been flooded with reactions concerning the reinstatement of the CEO. “Nvidia has REINSTATED Jensen Huang as CEO after his short break to play GTA 6,” a user posted. Another one noted: "Jensen just wanted some time off to complete Grand Theft Auto VI and spend some time with Mandy". A third one marked the CEO as the greatest of all time and said: “GOAT is back in the seat!” Here are a few more reactions from the platform.

Also Read: After Chinese spy link controversy, Nvidia reappoints Jensen Huang as CEO and president: All details

What Trump said on Jensen Huang’s comeback as Nvidia CEO

In a post on Truth Social, Trump lauded the move as the “correct and proper decision” . “I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure,” Trump added. Here is the official post:

Mistake Corrected: Elon Musk on Huang’s comeback

Reposting Nvidia’s X post concerning Huang’s reinstatement, the Tesla CEO said: “The mistake has been corrected.”

Jensen Huang comeback: What Nvidia said

In a press release today, the company said: “Effective immediately, Jensen Huang has been reinstated as President and CEO and member of the Board”. The BoD voted unanimously to reinstate him after a series of meetings and discussions. Nvidia also noted that based on the inputs received from the US government, Huang poses no risk to national security.

Also Read: Why Nvidia brought Jensen Huang back as CEO

“I am honoured to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” said Jensen Huang. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment,” he added. You can read more details about why Nvidia reappointed Huang as CEO in this article.
