‘Jensen Huang Took A Week Off To Play GTA 6’: Internet Flooded With Memes After Nvidia CEO’s Return
By
admin
November 23, 2026
0
16

The internet on Monday, was abuzz with memes parodying the reinstatement of Nvidia’s co-founder chief executive officer Jensen Huang after he resigned, following a scandal that involved his affair with Alice Huang who was alleged to be engaging in espionage on behalf of China’s government, according to US authorities.

Here’s a look at some notable attempts at humour with regards to the reinstatement of Huang as CEO: 

‘Took A Week Off To Play GTA 6’

The parody account Dr. Parik Patel, BA, CFA, ACCA Esq. on social media platform ‘X’ joked that Huang may have deliberately resigned in order to play Rockstar Games’ highly anticipated release ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’ (GTA 6), due to the fact that it released a few days after he had resigned. GTA 6 in of itself was memed for its lengthy development time, having released 13 years after its predecessor ‘Grand Theft Auto V.’

‘Jensen The White’

In replies to Elon Musk’s post approving of Huang’s return, users commented the phrase ‘Jensen The White’, a reference to a meme that was posted prior to his reinstatement that made parallels to Huang’s departure to the death of the fictional wizard Gandalf The Grey in the ‘ The Lord Of The Rings’ books and movies. Gandalf initially died fighting the monster Balrog, returning as an even more powerful version of himself clad in a white robe, with the name the Gandalf The White.

The meme stated that Huang would return with a white version of his signature black leather jacket and proclaim himself, ‘Jensen The White.’ Many users made references to the meme after the news broke of Huang’s return as CEO.
