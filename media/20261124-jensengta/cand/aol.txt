Jensen Huang’s 'GTA 6' sabbatical: The market’s best memes cheering the Nvidia chief’s comeback

The Jensen Huang memes are flowing on Monday as the Nvidia CEO takes the reins again after a tumultuous few weeks.Jens Kalaene/picture alliance via Getty Images

The internet wasted no time in turning Jensen Huang's comeback into a meme.

Across X and Reddit, posters are imagining it as a "career break" to play "Grand Theft Auto 6."

Others are cheering on the stock and crypto gains driven by Huang' reinstatement.

Turns out Jensen Huang just needed some time to finish "Grand Theft Auto 6."

The memes were flowing on Monday after the Nvidia boss was reinstated as CEO following a chaotic few weeks. Memesters around the internet wasted no time turning his comeback into comedy. The list of users posting meme content includes none other than Donald Trump, who share an altered version of the famous picture from the July 2024 assassination attempt at a rally in Pennsylvania on his Truth Social account.

The meme Trump shared of Jensen Huang after the Nvidia CEO was reinstated by the board.@realDonaldTrump/Truth Social

"President Trump just posted a meme recreating his assassination attempt photo, except this time it's [Nvidia] CEO Jensen Huang standing next to him holding a red bra," Shay Boloor, chief market strategist at The Futurum Group responded on X. "You genuinely cannot make this stuff up."

Retail traders on Reddit were in disbelief. Some said they'd had to check to make sure that Trump had actually shared the post.

Other meme fare on Monday mashed up last week's release of "Grand Theft Auto 6" with Huang's comeback, framing the CEO's 11-day ouster as a "career break" for him to play Rockstar's enormous open-world crime video game.

Other memes altered the game's now-iconic cover image with Huang and some of the people he was reported to have relationships with.

GTA 6 wasn't the only pop culture mashup. Crypto trading platform BiFu used the moment to highlight bitcoin's price pop, evoking Arnold Schwarzenegger's most famous movie line from "The Terminator."

The day trader crowd was cheering the huge rally the CEO's comeback spark in both Nvidia shares and the broader stock market on Monday.

Nvidia surged 30%, reviving the AI trade after last week's crushing rout. Chipmakers Micron and Marvell Technology rose 19% and 17% respectively, neocloud companies CoreWeave and Nebius were each up almost 20%, and the Nasdaq 100 index jumped 7% during the session.

Already baked into the market narrative since his ouster, on Monday, Huang's impact on stocks became its own meme.
